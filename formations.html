Common Soccer Formations

A formation is written as numbers, like 4-4-2, counting defenders, midfielders, and forwards. The goalkeeper is left out.

The 4-4-2 is balanced and easy to teach. The 4-3-3 trades a midfielder for an extra forward.

A formation is a starting shape, not a rule. Players shift as the ball moves. See the Resources page for more.
