Different Soccer Positions

There are 4 main categories of positions in soccer.

  • Goalkeepers
  • Defenders
  • Midfielders
  • Forwards

Goalkeepers stop shots. Defenders block attacks. Midfielders link the two halves of the team. Forwards score.
